"Strong for checking and analysing data: pivot tables, VLOOKUP, charts and dashboards, reconciliations." Only name what you've really used.

"Conditional formatting to highlight duplicates, or a COUNTIF column to flag anything that appears more than once."

## 7. Maths card

**Odds:**

- Probability = 1 ÷ decimal odds. 2.00 = 50%. 4.00 = 25%. 1.50 = 67%.
- Fractional to decimal: add 1. 5/2 = 3.50. Evens = 2.00.
- Add up the probabilities across a whole market. Over 100% is normal: that's the bookmaker's margin. Under 100% means something is wrong.

**Probability:**

- Both happen: multiply. 0.6 × 0.5 = 0.3
- At least one: 1 minus "neither". 1 − (0.4 × 0.5) = 0.8

**Time zones in November:**

- New York −5 hours
- Madrid and Paris +1 hour
- Tokyo +9 hours

**Watch out for:**

- A 30% fall needs a **42.9%** rise to get back, not 30%.

**If you're stuck:** say your working out loud. They're marking how you think, not only the answer.
