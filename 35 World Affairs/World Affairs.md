# World Affairs

> [!info] Snapshot
> **What this covers:** learning about world politics and the news: what's happening, why it matters and the background behind it.
> **Goals right now:** build a working picture of the big ongoing stories (UK politics, US, Europe, the Middle East, Ukraine and Russia, China, the global economy) and follow how they develop.
> **Chat:** use the **World Affairs** chat in Claude Code. See [[Which chat for what]].

## How it works
- **Briefings** (`Briefings/`): one note per briefing, named `YYYY-MM-DD Briefing.md`. Ask "give me today's briefing" or "what's been going on this week". Claude checks the news on the web and cites a source for every story.
- **Explainers** (`Explainers/`): one note per topic that keeps coming up, for example `Explainers/Ukraine war.md`. Background, key people, timeline, what to watch. Updated with a dated line in its Status log as the story moves.
- **Questions:** jot anything you want explained under Questions below and ask Claude to go through them.

## Rules for Claude
- Check the news on the web every time. Don't rely on training data for anything recent, and give the date of each story.
- Cite sources with links. Prefer a mix (BBC, Reuters, AP, the Guardian, the FT, the Economist) and say when outlets disagree.
- Neutral and plain. Explain each side's case fairly, separate fact from opinion, flag what's uncertain.
- Assume no background knowledge: define terms and say who people are the first time.
- Summarise in your own words. Never paste articles.
- Where a story links to Bradley's job hunt (markets, sport, betting regulation, data and AI), say so in a line.

## Following
- **US politics** (main focus): the White House, Congress, the courts, elections and big policy fights.
- **Israel and Palestine** (main focus): the war in Gaza, the West Bank, ceasefire and hostage talks, and the wider region.
