It seems that certain photos in your listings may be someone else's **copyrighted images**, which goes against our Catalogue Rules.

These types of photos can't be used for listings on Vinted:
- Photos saved from the internet
- Stock photos
- Screenshots from other websites (except invoices, which can't be the main image or the only one)
- Photos taken by other members

Instead, make sure to **use photos you've taken yourself.**

**To make these hidden listings visible again, please add new photos** that you've taken yourself and remove any photos that go against the rules. These listings will remain hidden until you make the updates.
