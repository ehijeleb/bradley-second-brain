# Which chat for what

Each area of the brain has its own chat in Claude Code, filed under **Bradley's brain** in the sidebar. They all work on this same vault, so anything one chat changes, the others can see. Pick the chat by topic:

| Chat | Use it for | Hubs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Career** | Job adverts, applications, follow-ups, CVs, cover letters, interview prep, recruiters | [[Job Hunt]], [[CV and Interview Prep]], [[Contacts and Recruiters]] |
| **Learning and Portfolio** | Courses, skills, portfolio projects, Tableau and Power BI work | [[Skills Roadmap]], [[Portfolio Projects]] |
| **Reselling** | Buying, listing and selling, stock, expenses, profit. Also tracks personal finances: spending, income, budget (added 15-11-2026) | [[Reselling]], [[Stock]], [[Reselling expenses]], [[House of Resell]], [[Finance]] |
| **Bershka and Money** | Shifts and rota, pay and payslips, budget, bills, tax | [[Bershka]], [[Finance]] |
| **Personal** | Gym and fitness, admin, calendar `.ics` files, emails in `00 Inbox` | [[Fitness]], [[Admin]] |
| **World Affairs** | News briefings, world politics, explainers, questions about what's going on | [[World Affairs]] |
| **Daily plan** | Runs by itself at 8am and writes the day's plan into today's daily note. Reply in that chat to adjust the day. | `01 Daily` |

Not sure which? Use the area the thing mostly belongs to. A weekly review can go in any chat.

## Cloud chats (phone and web)
Each area also has a cloud chat, set up 17-11-2026, for when the laptop is off. Open claude.ai/code or the Claude app and pick it by name: **Career (cloud)**, **Learning and Portfolio (cloud)**, **Reselling (cloud)**, **Bershka and Money (cloud)**, **Personal (cloud)**, **World Affairs (cloud)**. Same areas and hubs as the table above. They work on the GitHub copy of the vault and push to `main`, so changes reach the laptop and iPhone through Obsidian Git. The daily plan stays on the laptop.

## Tips
- Chats get slow when very long. If one does, start a new chat in the same group and say which area it's for.
- The daily plan needs the Claude app open on the laptop. If the laptop is off at 8am, it runs the next time the app opens.
